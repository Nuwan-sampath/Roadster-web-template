02 — ENGINEERING

Built like
a spacecraft.

  • Dual-motor RWD, torque vectoring

    One motor per rear wheel on an 800–1,000 V pack. Over 1,000 kW. The 2017 tri-motor AWD plan is gone — this is the new recipe.

  • Multi-mode suction fan

    Pulls air from under the car — enough downforce to hold it upside down, even parked. 2 g cornering claimed. Press T for Track mode.

  • Aerospace materials

    Magnesium, titanium, carbon fiber. Target weight under 1,700 kg. Ano White paint borrowed from rocket protection tech.

  • Yoke + gold visor glass

    Steer-by-wire yoke, ultra-white interior, and tinted glass that mimics the gold coating on spacesuit visors. Open the Studio (C) to explode the car and inspect every part.

03 — SPACEX PACKAGE, BUT REAL

Yes, it
actually flies.

The Thrust trim hides four rear nozzles fed by a 200-litre nitrogen tank at 10,000 psi — about five seconds of thrust per fill. The invite-only Rocket trim adds six more around the perimeter. It hops. It barrel-rolls. It is not street legal.

06 — THE LONG ROAD

Nine years
of “next year”.

  • NOV 2017

    The original promise

    Driven out of a Semi trailer: 1.9 s 0–60, 620 mi range, 200 kWh, tri-motor AWD, 2+2 seats, glass roof. $200k base, $250k Founders.

  • 2018 → 2025

    The SpaceX tease & the delays

    Cold-gas thruster option announced. Production slipped: 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025… then “April 1, 2026.”

  • 15 OCT 2026

    McGregor, Texas

    A driverless Roadster with a spacesuit-clad mannequin drove upside down, barrel-rolled over other Teslas, and landed. New design, three trims, 0.81 s measured.

  • 2028

    Production target — again

    Giga Texas. $50,000 refundable deposit. Price still unannounced. “Success is not guaranteed,” Musk admitted before the demo.
